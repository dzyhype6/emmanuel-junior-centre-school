About Our School

Built on Faith, Excellence & Community

Emmanuel Junior Centre School is a registered CBC school in Nairobi, Kenya, serving families at Kayole Junction. Our two campuses operate side by side to carry learners from Playgroup all the way through Grade 9.

🏫 EJCS — Primary School, Playgroup–Grade 6 🎓 EJSS — Junior Secondary, Grade 7–9 (CBC Rated) 📍 P.O. Box 75904-0200, Nairobi · Kayole Junction
